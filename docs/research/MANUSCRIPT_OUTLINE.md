# Conditional manuscript outline — not a draft

Working concept: Evidence Contracts for Risk-Bounded Agentic Investigation of Privileged-Access Audit Logs. The title is contingent on future evidence and disclosure clearance.

1. **Problem and permitted abstraction.** Define one bounded privileged-access investigation task, its evidence obligations and analyst decision. Exclude customer identities, deployment and production outcomes.
2. **Related work and claim boundary.** Compare the final contract/gateway/verification mechanism with primary research on agent tool use, evidence attribution, prompt injection, abstention and selective prediction. Establish whether any combination provides a measurable contribution.
3. **System.** Immutable canonical events, explicit hypothesis, provenance graph, evidence representations, capability gateway, external deadline and isolated semantic verifier. Separate deterministic guarantees from probabilistic judgments and administrator trust assumptions.
4. **Task and datasets.** Generator, source/label separation, negative and partial cases, independent adjudication, leakage threats, grouping, real-world gap and allowed release scope. Do not present current synthetic frequencies as prevalence.
5. **Protocol.** Six baselines, full component/representation/budget ablations, pinned models, frozen development/test/challenge splits, provider usage accounting, failure retention and independent labeling criteria.
6. **Results.** Only mechanically regenerated tables with explicit numerators/denominators. Separate event/edge/path retrieval, citation existence, semantic support, contradiction/coverage, selective risk, review workload, efficiency and attack goals.
7. **Failure analysis.** Incorrect joins, missing evidence, misleading semantics, unsupported intent, injection, truncation and timeout; review both prevented false positives and missed legitimate patterns.
8. **Limitations and ethics.** Synthetic dependence, provider drift, uncalibrated policies, same-model error correlation, excluded infrastructure threats, company IP/privacy and lack of analyst validation.
9. **Reproducibility appendix.** Exact approved code/data/prompt hashes, environment, commands, raw runs and immutable manifests. Release only materials cleared in writing.
